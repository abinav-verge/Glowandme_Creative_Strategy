## Real Customer Language
- "This dark spot will never go away." — Why this phrase matters: The finality of "never" is emotionally charged and copy-ready. It captures the exact hopelessness the avatar feels — and a headline that begins "What if that dark spot CAN go away?" speaks directly to this resignation and flips it into possibility.
- "dark patches" — Why this phrase matters: This is the most common, natural descriptor used across multiple posts and platforms. Not "hyperpigmentation" (clinical), not "discoloration" (vague) — "dark patches" is the vernacular. Any copy or product naming that uses this exact phrase will achieve instant recognition.
- "won't fade at all" — Why this phrase matters: "Fade" is the aspirational verb this community uses. They don't say "disappear" or "cure" — they say "fade." This is the language of gradual, believable progress. Copy promising to "fade" dark spots will feel more credible than copy promising to "eliminate" them.
- "stubborn dark spots" — Why this phrase matters: "Stubborn" is deeply humanizing — it assigns personality to the problem, making it feel like a battle between the person and their skin. This word validates the struggle and signals that the solution must be specifically designed to overcome resistance, not just treat ordinary spots.
- "Symmetrical dark patches on both sides of face" — Why this phrase matters: The specificity of this description — symmetrical, bilateral — shows that avatars notice and communicate precise visual details. Copy that speaks to the specific location and pattern of their concern (not just generic "dark spots") will feel like it was written for them.
- "Is it treatable?" — Why this phrase matters: These three words are the rawest expression of the avatar's emotional state. Before benefits, before ingredients, before price — they need to know if hope is even on the table. Any marketing that leads with "Yes, it's treatable — and here's proof" will resonate at the most fundamental emotional level.
- "minimal but effective routine" — Why this phrase matters: This phrase reveals the avatar's relationship with solutions — they want results without overwhelm. Copy that promises simplicity alongside efficacy will lower the barrier to commitment and speak directly to their action-fatigue.
